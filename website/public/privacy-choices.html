Privacy choices.

on-air remote collects no data. Your phone talks only to the on-air companion on your own computer, over your local network. There is no on-air account, server, analytics, or advertising, so there is nothing for us to opt you out of, export, or delete. Every control below lives on your own devices.

Local network access

on-air remote needs Local Network permission to find and control your desktop. To turn it off, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network and switch off on-air remote. The remote cannot reach your desktop until you turn it back on.

Saved pairing on your iPhone or iPad

In the app, open Connection and tap “Forget desktop” to remove the saved desktop address, port, and pairing token from the device’s Keychain. iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so use Forget desktop first if you want the pairing removed from the device.

Paired phones on your computer

The companion keeps hashed pairing tokens in paired-remotes.json beside its settings, so paired phones still work after a restart. To revoke every paired phone, quit the companion, delete that file, and start the companion again. Each phone then needs the new PIN to pair.

  • macOS desktop app: ~/Library/Application Support/com.shay.on-air/
  • Linux desktop app: ~/.config/com.shay.on-air/
  • Windows desktop app: %APPDATA%\com.shay.on-air\
  • Headless core: ~/.config/on-air-core/, or the folder set by ON_AIR_CONFIG_DIR

Diagnostics shared with Apple

Apple may offer crash and usage diagnostics from your device to developers, if you allow it. Manage this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Share With App Developers.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising, so there are no tracking preferences to manage. How the hosting provider handles requests is described in the privacy policy.
