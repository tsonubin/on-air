Privacy policy.

on-air remote is a local-network controller for the on-air desktop companion. The app does not require an on-air account, and does not send your personal data to an on-air server.

What stays on your device

After pairing, the desktop address, port, and pairing token are saved using the device’s secure storage. On iOS, this uses the Keychain with access limited to this device while it is unlocked. Use “Forget desktop” in Connection to remove the saved pairing from this app.

What your desktop receives

The app discovers computers on your local network and communicates directly with the desktop you select. Pairing sends the PIN you enter to that desktop, which returns a token for future connections. Mixer commands and status updates travel between your device and desktop. The desktop stores pairing-token hashes so it can recognize authorized remotes after restarting.

Local network permission

Local network access lets the app find and control your on-air desktop. Keep the phone and desktop on a trusted local network. The companion’s control API is intended for local use and should not be exposed directly to the internet.

Tracking, advertising, and analytics

The app contains no advertising, tracking SDK, or on-air analytics service. We do not collect app usage data or sell personal information. Apple may separately provide diagnostics under your device’s privacy settings.

This website and external links

This site does not add advertising or analytics scripts, cookies, or a contact form. Its hosting provider processes requests needed to serve the pages, including network information such as IP addresses. Hosting-provider logs are governed by that provider’s policy. Links to GitHub and Apple are governed by those services’ own privacy policies. Information you choose to post in a public GitHub issue is public; do not include pairing tokens or private network information.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the on-air issue tracker. Source code for the app and companion is available on GitHub.
